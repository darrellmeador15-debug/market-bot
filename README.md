# Market Bot

AI stock market scanner, backtesting, strategy testing, and Schwab trading system.

## Goals

- Scan stocks for momentum, volume, news, and price action.
- Test trading strategies with historical data.
- Detect early momentum and breakout setups.
- Backtest across different market conditions.
- Connect to Schwab for paper trading and, later, controlled execution.
- Continuously test and improve strategies based on measured results.
